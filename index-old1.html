About

Fin is a Manager in Ampol’s Group Strategy & Corporate Development team. He executes M&A, strategy and portfolio optimisation initiatives across Ampol’s energy and infrastructure businesses, and prepares executive, Investment Committee and Board materials for strategic transactions.

Before Ampol, Fin spent six years at EY, most of it in Transaction Diligence across Sydney and New York. He ran buy-side and sell-side financial due diligence for private equity and corporate clients, covering adjusted EBITDA, working capital, net debt and SPA/PPA review.

Outside work: humanitarian projects in Cambodia, Operation Christmas Child and Oxfam Trailwalker, plus tennis, rugby, touch football, cricket and golf.
